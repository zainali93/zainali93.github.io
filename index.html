ABOUT

Researching language technologies, from multilingual NLP to LLMs.

I am an AI/NLP researcher and PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Waikato, New Zealand. My research spans natural language processing, large language models, and deep learning, with particular experience in multilingual and low-resource language technologies.

My work in NLP began with sentiment and emotion analysis of social-media text and has since expanded across misinformation detection, machine-generated text detection, stance detection, and large language model adaptation. During my PhD, I have explored approaches ranging from domain-adaptive pretraining and context-aware stance detection to adapting existing LLMs through tokenizer replacement, continual pretraining, and knowledge distillation.

Alongside academic research, I have experience developing applied NLP and machine-learning systems and teaching engineering computing and machine learning. I am interested in developing robust and practical language technologies, particularly where advances in NLP and deep learning can improve the capabilities of multilingual and underrepresented-language systems.

RESEARCH

Research Areas

01

Large Language Models

Adapting pretrained LLMs to Urdu through tokenizer replacement, continual pretraining, knowledge elicitation, and knowledge distillation.

02

Low-Resource & Urdu NLP

Developing and adapting language technologies for Urdu under limited linguistic, data and computational resources, including pretrained language models and downstream NLP applications.

03

Misinformation & Machine-Generated Text

Investigating misinformation through text classification, contextual stance detection, and the joint analysis of content veracity and human or machine authorship, particularly in Urdu.

04

Sentiment & Emotion Analysis

Earlier research on sentiment and emotion classification in social-media text, including Urdu sentiment analysis and applications to epidemic surveillance and aspect-based analysis.
